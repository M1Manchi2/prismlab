StageMask (屏隐替身) Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective Date / 生效日期：2026-11-24

1. Zero Data Collection / 零数据收集承诺

StageMask processes all webpage text detection, fake demo-data replacement, element blurring, screenshot framing, and Pro license verification 100% locally inside your browser. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell your browsing history, webpage contents, screenshots, or personal data.

屏隐替身的所有敏感词识别、仿真替身替换、网页打码、脱敏截图与 Pro 激活码校验均 100% 在用户浏览器本地离线完成。本扩展不收集、不上传、不存储、不出售任何浏览记录、网页内容或个人敏感信息。

2. Technical Guarantee / 技术与权限保障

  • Extension pages enforce Content Security Policy connect-src 'none', structurally blocking outbound network requests from the extension UI.
  • storage permission is used solely to save your local masking preferences, per-domain rules, and offline-verified Pro license key on your own device.
  • activeTab permission is used solely when you click the screenshot button to capture the visible tab locally into a PNG file saved directly to your device.
  • License verification uses local Ed25519 cryptographic signature checks with zero license-server telemetry.

3. Third-Party Payments / 第三方收款平台

When you optionally choose to purchase a Pro Lifetime license key via external checkout links (such as Mianbaoduo or Creem), payment processing is handled directly by those platforms under their respective privacy policies. The extension itself never sees your payment card or billing details.

4. Acceptable Use & Legal Compliance / 合法使用声明

StageMask is strictly designed for privacy protection during legitimate screen sharing, tutorials, and product demos. Users are strictly prohibited from using this extension to forge financial statements, payment receipts, identity documents, or misleading commercial claims. To prevent abuse, built-in currency obfuscation is restricted to downward scaling only (0.05×–0.80×).

屏隐替身仅用于合法的屏幕共享、教学演示与办公隐私防泄露场景。严禁将本工具用于伪造财务流水、交易凭证、身份信息或虚假宣传。为防止滥用，内置金额脱敏仅支持等比缩减（0.05×–0.80×），禁止放大数值。

5. Contact / 联系方式

For privacy inquiries or support, please use the support contact listed on the official Microsoft Edge Add-ons / Chrome Web Store listing page.
